Su Wudu Desktop Pet includes the official OpenAI Codex CLI binary distribution.

Component: OpenAI Codex CLI
Version: 0.144.4
Source: https://github.com/openai/codex
License: Apache License 2.0
License text: https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/main/LICENSE

The Codex component is used locally as an app-server bridge so users can sign in
with ChatGPT through OpenAI's OAuth flow. Su Wudu Desktop Pet never asks for or
stores the user's ChatGPT password.
